== Description ==

Chatway is a WordPress live chat, AI chatbot, and helpdesk plugin for customer support. Chat with website visitors in real time, automate replies with an AI chatbot, or connect your own AI agents including ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Muse, Cursor, Windsurf, Codex, and custom AI agents.

Add FAQ, WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, and other chat buttons to your website. Manage customer conversations in one live chat inbox while AI support agents handle repetitive questions and human agents handle complex issues.

3. You're good to go! The Chatway live chat widget will appear on your WordPress website 🚀
